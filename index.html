Platform modules

Four proprietary systems that power the AAMush mesh.

AURA Routing

AI-orchestrated convoy contracts that circularize multi-leg routes and guarantee backhaul for independent truck owners.

REGEN Registry

Three rare digital registries — dynamic warehouse receipt NFTs, zero-knowledge DIDs, and AI-predicted customs pre-clearance.

SURETY Settlement

Unified command layer with predictive commodity pricing, climate risk overlays, and omni-ledger settlement across fiat, stablecoin, and CBDC.

NEXUS Ghost Inventory

Bid on future harvests 30–90 days pre-harvest, backed by satellite-verified field data and weather derivatives.

Idle-Capacity Staking

Truck owners lock telematics into the mesh and receive instant liquidity advances — 60% of predicted earnings before the engine starts.

Cross-Border Trust

Trustless transactions across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi, DRC, and Ethiopia — no cash-on-delivery fraud exposure.
