INVESTOR REFERENCE

One-Minute Explanation

AAMush Mesh-01 is the physical device that connects the real movement of goods and trucks to the AAMush digital trade-logistics network.

It collects and authenticates telemetry, converts physical activity into structured logistics events, stores those events when networks are unavailable, and synchronizes them with the AAMush mesh.

Those events can support AURA routing and convoy management, REGEN digital registry functions, SURETY commercial settlement workflows and NEXUS predictive inventory.

Core Value

Mesh-01 is intended to bridge the physical economy and the digital trade network.
