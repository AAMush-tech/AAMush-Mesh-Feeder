PART I — INVENTION AND SYSTEM DEFINITION

1. INTRODUCTION

AAMush Mesh-01 is designed as a physical-edge feeder device forming the connection between the physical world of transportation, commodities, warehouses, vehicles and logistics operations and the digital AAMush autonomous trade-logistics mesh.

The device is intended to continuously or periodically acquire authorized operational information from a vehicle or other logistics asset, convert that information into structured digital events, authenticate and protect those events, retain them when communications are unavailable, and transmit them to the AAMush network when connectivity is available.

2. THE AAMUSH CONCEPT

AAMush is the developer-defined identity of the technology platform.

Within the technology architecture, AAMush represents the integrated ecosystem through which physical logistics, digital identity, predictive intelligence, transport capacity, commodity information and commercial settlement can operate as a connected system.

Mesh-01 is the physical feeder component of this larger ecosystem.

3. WHAT IS AAMUSH MESH-01?

AAMush Mesh-01 is an intelligent logistics-edge device designed to:

  • identify a registered logistics asset;
  • acquire telemetry;
  • determine operational state;
  • record location and movement information;
  • generate structured logistics events;
  • timestamp events;
  • preserve event sequence;
  • store events locally;
  • communicate with the AAMush mesh;
  • synchronize events;
  • report device health;
  • support route and convoy monitoring;
  • provide physical-world evidence to authorized AAMush services;
  • support logistics contracts;
  • support cargo and warehouse workflows;
  • support cross-border logistics;
  • support commercial and settlement workflows;
  • support future AI and predictive logistics functions.

4. THE CORE INVENTIVE PRINCIPLE

Physical logistics activity can be converted into authenticated digital events that can participate in an integrated logistics, identity, inventory, routing and settlement environment.

Vehicle ↔ Telemetry ↔ Mesh Event ↔ Digital Identity ↔ Route/Convoy ↔ Cargo ↔ Warehouse ↔ Contract ↔ Settlement

5. PROBLEM THE INVENTION ADDRESSES

Traditional logistics systems frequently operate as disconnected components. A truck may have a GPS tracker. A warehouse may have an inventory system. A trader may have a purchase contract. A bank may have a financing system. Customs may have another system. Drivers may use mobile phones and commodity producers may have agricultural data.

These systems can produce fragmented information. AAMush is designed to connect these elements through a common logistics-event architecture.

Mesh-01 provides the physical-world entry point.

  2. STATE 01 — Provisioned: Device identity and security credentials have been assigned.
  3. STATE 02 — Registered: Device has been registered within AAMush.
  4. STATE 03 — Installed: Device has been physically installed on a vehicle or logistics asset.
  5. STATE 04 — Commissioned: Device has passed installation and communications tests.
  6. STATE 05 — Operational: Device is actively acquiring and transmitting approved telemetry.
  7. STATE 06 — Offline: Device remains operational locally but cannot currently communicate with the network.
  8. STATE 07 — Synchronizing: Previously stored events are being transmitted.
  9. STATE 08 — Maintenance: Device has been placed into an authorized maintenance state.
  10. STATE 09 — Suspended: Device is prevented from participating in normal operations.
  11. STATE 10 — Reassigned: Device identity remains intact while its vehicle/operator association changes.
  12. STATE 11 — Decommissioned: Device has been permanently removed from service.

8. MESH-01 END-TO-END OPERATION

  1. IDENTIFY — Mesh-01 identifies itself to the AAMush platform.
  2. CONNECT — Establish an authorized communications path.
  3. ACQUIRE — Receive telemetry from GNSS, vehicle systems and/or approved sensors.
  4. PROCESS — Interpret incoming information.
  5. GENERATE EVENT — Create a structured logistics event.
  6. AUTHENTICATE — Associate the event with device identity and integrity information.
  7. STORE — Store the event locally until successful synchronization.
  8. TRANSMIT — Send the event to the AAMush network.
  9. ACKNOWLEDGE — Network confirms receipt.
  10. SYNCHRONIZE — Mesh-01 records synchronization.
  11. INTERPRET — Platform services use the event according to authorized workflows.
  12. ACT — A route, convoy, warehouse, inventory, financing, settlement or other workflow may respond.

24. MEMORY AND LOCAL STORAGE

Mesh-01 shall contain sufficient non-volatile storage for operational event retention during network failure, temporary power interruption, server unavailability, communications instability and delayed synchronization.

25. GNSS SUBSYSTEM

The GNSS subsystem provides location information for route tracking, geofencing, ETA calculation, convoy monitoring, border events, warehouse arrival, departure and route deviation.

26. COMMUNICATIONS SUBSYSTEM

The final architecture may support cellular, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, local mesh radio, satellite or other approved connectivity.

Connection → Authentication → Transmission → Acknowledgement → Synchronization

28. VEHICLE INTERFACE

PART VIII — FAILURE MANAGEMENT

78. NETWORK FAILURE

DO NOT ERASE LOCAL EVENTS.

Detect failure → continue local operation → store events → retry communication → synchronize when restored.

79. GNSS FAILURE

Record GNSS failure, preserve last valid position, continue other telemetry where possible, attempt recovery and record recovery.

80. POWER FAILURE

Record the power event where possible, preserve unsynchronized data, shut down safely if supported, restart when power returns, verify configuration and synchronize outstanding events.

81. DEVICE FAILURE

Identify device, record failure, preserve available data, repair or replace device, recommission, verify identity, restore vehicle association and test synchronization.

82. DEVICE THEFT

Suspend device identity, revoke credentials if necessary, record incident, prevent unauthorized platform access, replace device and maintain historical continuity.

106. WHY IT IS NOT JUST A GPS TRACKER

A conventional tracker primarily answers: “Where is the vehicle?”

Mesh-01 is intended to answer:

  • Which vehicle is this?
  • Who is authorized to operate it?
  • Where is it?
  • What is it doing?
  • Which route is it performing?
  • Which convoy is it part of?
  • Which cargo or contract is associated with it?
  • Has it reached the warehouse?
  • Has the physical logistics condition occurred?
  • Has the event been synchronized?
  • Can the event be used by an authorized commercial workflow?

125. FINAL SYSTEM DEFINITION

AAMush Mesh-01 is an intelligent physical-edge feeder device and logistics gateway designed to acquire, process, authenticate, preserve, communicate and synchronize physical-world logistics information with an autonomous commodity and trade-logistics mesh.

The system is intended to enable physical logistics events to participate in authorized digital workflows involving transportation, convoy coordination, inventory, warehouse records, identity, predictive commodity supply, financing/advance mechanisms and settlement.
