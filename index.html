Platform Identity

AAMush Trade Intelligence Grid™ is a regional digital infrastructure designed to connect physical trade movement with cargo, corridor, border and predictive intelligence across East Africa.

The platform operates across six strategic nodes: Dar es Salaam, Mombasa, Nairobi, Kampala, Kigali and Bujumbura — with Dodoma as the Central Corridor hub.

Mesh-01 Physical Layer

Mesh-01 is the telemetry and device infrastructure of the AAMush Trade Intelligence Grid™. It captures authenticated data from participating vehicles and assets.

Telemetry captured:

• GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, altitude)
• Speed and heading
• Timestamp (UTC + EAT)
• Connectivity status (GSM, satellite, mesh relay)
• Device health (battery, firmware, signal strength)

Authentication: Each Mesh-01 device is cryptographically paired to a registered asset. Data is signed at the edge before transmission.

Developed by Dr. James K. Mushi, PhD • AAMush Inc.

Four-Layer Architecture

1. Physical Layer — Mesh-01
Captures authenticated telemetry from vehicles/assets.

2. Data Layer — Integration Fabric
Aggregates Mesh-01, port, customs, logistics and border feeds.

3. Intelligence Layer — Engine
Calculates ETA, detects anomalies, predicts bottlenecks, generates alerts.

4. Command Layer — AAMush Live
Visualises the six-node network, displays real-time intelligence, enables decision-making.

Investor Proposition

The Problem: East African trade generates enormous operational information, but it is fragmented across actors and systems.

The Solution: AAMush creates an intelligence layer above those systems — transforming fragmented data into visibility, measurement, prediction, decision and intervention.

Economic Logic: Less uncertainty → fewer avoidable delays → better asset utilisation → more predictable trade → stronger regional economic activity.

Revenue Streams: Hardware (Mesh-01), SaaS subscriptions, government command centres, corridor analytics, enterprise APIs, digital-twin modelling.

Technical Specifications

Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, SVG, vanilla JavaScript. No framework dependencies for prototype.
